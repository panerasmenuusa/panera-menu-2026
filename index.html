    Full Panera menu guide

    The Panera menu, section by section

    🍳 Breakfast menu🥪 Sandwiches🍲 Soup menu🥗 Salads⭐ Value menu🥐 Bakery☕ Drinks🧒 Kids & families💡 Ordering tips📋 Full price list

    The Panera menu is built around bread baked in the cafe. Bagel stacks at breakfast, toasted sandwiches at lunch and sourdough bread bowls filled with soup all start there. Below is the complete Panera menu for 2026, with what each item costs, what is worth ordering and how to pay less.

    🍳

    Panera breakfast menu

    from $3.89

    The Panera breakfast menu is led by its bagel stacks. The Wake-Up BLT Asiago Everything Bagel Stack, the Farmhouse Duo and the Sausage & Egg Asiago Bagel Stack are each about $6.99. So are the Steak & Wake and the two baked soufflés, Spinach & Bacon and Four Cheese. For a classic egg sandwich, the ciabatta and croissant Bacon, Sausage or Ham, Egg & Cheese options cost about $6.89, and the plain Egg & Cheese about $5.39.

    Lighter morning choices on the Panera menu include the Garden Avo & Egg White Sandwich (about $6.89), Classic Avo toast (about $3.89), Steel Cut Oatmeal with Strawberries & Pecans (about $6.19) and the Greek Yogurt with Berries Parfait (about $5.89). Add a bagel for about $1.99, or a hot Cafe Blend coffee for about $2.99.

    🥪

    Panera sandwiches menu

    $7.99 – $13.39

    Sandwiches are the centre of the Panera lunch menu, and most come on Panera's own breads: ciabatta, focaccia, sourdough and the black pepper focaccia. Hot, toasted favourites include:

    • Toasted Frontega Chicken$11.79
    • Chipotle Chicken Avo Melt$11.99
    • Chicken Bacon Rancher$11.99
    • Toasted Italiano$12.99
    • French Ciabatta Dipper$12.49
    • Ciabatta Cheesesteak$13.39

    On the cold side, the Bacon Turkey Bravo (about $11.99) is a long-time fan favourite. It is joined by the Grilled Chicken & Avo BLT ($12.99), Turkey & Cheddar ($10.29), Tuna Salad ($10.39) and Cranberry Walnut Chicken Salad ($9.49). The cheapest sandwiches on the Panera menu are the Mediterranean Veggie and the classic Grilled Cheese, both about $7.99. Builds, breads and photos for every sandwich are on the Panera Sandwiches Menu page.

    Panera soup menu and bread bowls

    $8.49 · bread bowl $9.08

    Soup is what many people picture when they think of the Panera menu. Every soup costs about $8.49:

    Broccoli CheddarCreamy TomatoHomestyle Chicken NoodleBistro French OnionCream of Chicken & Wild RiceRustic Baked PotatoHearty Fireside ChiliAutumn Squash (seasonal)

    Order most of them in a sourdough bread bowl for about $9.08, which is only about 59¢ more and the best-value upgrade on the menu. Broccoli Cheddar in a bread bowl is the classic order. Mac & Cheese sits in the same section at about $10.59, or $11.79 with bacon. For groups, soups also come by the quart for about $21.99. Seasonal soups, sizes and group quarts are listed on the Panera Bread Soup Menu page.

    Panera salads menu

    $8.99 – $13.59

    Panera salads are full meals. The Green Goddess Chicken Cobb tops the range at about $13.59. The Harvest Medley Chicken and Southwest Chicken Ranch are about $12.99. Most other chicken salads cost about $11.99, including the Fuji Apple, Asian Sesame, Caesar with Chicken and Greek with Chicken. The Greens with Grains bowls (Mediterranean and Balsamic) cost about $12.99 with chicken or $9.99 without. For the lowest price, a plain Caesar or Greek salad is about $8.99.

    Panera bakery menu and bagel deals

    from $1.99

    The bakery case is the cheapest part of the Panera menu. Single bagels, including Asiago Everything, Cinnamon Crunch, Plain, Blueberry and Sesame, are about $1.99. A Half Dozen is about $9.29 and a Baker's Dozen (13 bagels) about $13.99. On Tuesdays many bakery-cafes sell a Baker's Dozen for about $9.99, which is under 77¢ a bagel. Our bagel deal finder works out the cheapest mix for any number.

    Sweets include the Chocolate Chipper Cookie (about $3.19), the oversized Kitchen Sink Cookie ($4.89) and the Brownie ($3.99). Pastries such as the Cinnamon Roll, Pecan Braid, Chocolate Croissant and Cherry Pastry are about $4.39. Muffins and scones are about $3.89. Whole loaves are on sale too: a French Baguette is about $3.99 and a Country Rustic Sourdough about $8.79.

    Panera drinks menu and the Unlimited Sip Club

    $2.49 – $6.49

    Fountain drinks, Iced Tea and Agave Lemonade cost about $3.39. The Blueberry Lavender and Strawberry Basil lemonades and the Cherry Lime fresca are about $4.29, hot coffee is about $2.99, lattes are $4.59–$5.19 and fruit smoothies are about $6.49. Milk and organic apple juice are about $2.49.

    The Unlimited Sip Club costs about $14.99 a month or $99 a year for eligible drinks. At $3.39 a drink, the monthly plan pays for itself at five drinks a month, roughly one per week. Try your own numbers in the Sip Club calculator above.

    Panera kids menu and family meals

    kids from $5.79

    The Panera kids menu has Grilled Cheese (about $5.79), Broccoli Cheddar Soup ($6.79), Mac & Cheese ($6.99) and a Turkey or Ham & Cheese sandwich ($7.19). For a family dinner, the Family Feast is about $36, or $41 with cookies. The Premium Family Feast is about $41, or $46 with cookies. The Egg Sandwich Feast covers breakfast for a group at about $15.99.

    💡

    How to get the most from the Panera menu

    1. Start with Value Duets. A sandwich-and-soup pair costs less than most single sandwiches.
    2. Upgrade to a bread bowl. For about 59¢ more you get a whole sourdough bowl.
    3. Buy bagels on Tuesday. The Baker's Dozen deal cuts the price by more than a quarter.
    4. Do the Sip Club maths. About five drinks a month covers the monthly plan.
    5. Order ahead for pickup. Delivery apps often charge more per item, plus fees.
    6. Join MyPanera. The free rewards program gives members offers and birthday treats.

    Panera bakery-cafes are run by the company and by franchise partners, so prices follow local costs. Airports, city centres and delivery apps usually cost the most. The prices on this page are typical U.S. prices, last checked in November 2026.

    FAQ

    Panera menu questions

    Quick answers about Panera prices, deals and menu items in 2026.

    What is on the Panera menu?

    The Panera menu has breakfast bagel stacks, egg sandwiches and soufflés, hot and cold sandwiches, soups and bread bowls, Mac & Cheese, salads, Value Duets, bakery items like bagels, cookies and pastries, a kids menu and drinks such as lemonades, coffee and smoothies.

    How much is Panera in 2026?

    In 2026 most Panera sandwiches cost about $7.99 to $13.39, soups about $8.49, bread bowls about $9.08, entree salads about $8.99 to $13.59 and Value Duets about $7.69 to $7.99. Prices vary by bakery-cafe.

    What is the most popular item on the Panera menu?

    Fan favourites include the Bacon Turkey Bravo sandwich (about $11.99), Broccoli Cheddar Soup in a bread bowl (about $9.08), the Toasted Frontega Chicken (about $11.79) and Mac & Cheese (about $10.59).

    Does Panera have a value menu?

    Yes. Panera Value Duets pair a smaller sandwich or salad with a soup for about $7.99, such as Grilled Cheese with Creamy Tomato Soup.

    How much is a bread bowl at Panera?

    A soup in a sourdough bread bowl costs about $9.08, compared with about $8.49 for the soup alone.

    How much is a dozen bagels at Panera?

    A Baker's Dozen (13 bagels) is about $13.99, a Half Dozen about $9.29 and a single bagel about $1.99. On Tuesdays many bakery-cafes offer a Baker's Dozen for about $9.99.

    Is the Panera Unlimited Sip Club worth it?

    It costs about $14.99 a month or $99 a year. If you buy about five or more eligible drinks a month at around $3.39 each, the monthly plan pays for itself.

    Does Panera have a kids menu?

    Yes. The Panera kids menu includes Grilled Cheese (about $5.79), Broccoli Cheddar Soup ($6.79), Mac & Cheese ($6.99) and Turkey or Ham & Cheese sandwiches ($7.19).

    Is this the official Panera website?

    No. This is an independent, unofficial Panera menu guide. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Panera Bread.
